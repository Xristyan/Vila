Setting & location

Mountain Villa sits on a forested ridge above the valley floor, with uninterrupted views of snow-capped peaks from the main living level and primary suite. The property is reached by a private paved drive, set back from the road for complete seclusion while remaining a short drive from village dining, trailheads, and seasonal recreation.

Address: REPLACE: 1234 Alpine Ridge Road, Aspen, CO 81611
Elevation: REPLACE: 8,200 ft · Nearest town: REPLACE: 12 min to village center

Accommodations & sleeping

The home sleeps up to 10 guests across 4 bedrooms and 3.5 baths. The primary suite features a king bed, soaking tub, and private balcony; two guest rooms offer queen beds; a bunk room is ideal for families. Premium linens, blackout shades, and individually controlled heating keep every room comfortable through changing mountain weather.

  • Primary suite with ensuite & balcony
  • Two queen guest bedrooms
  • Family bunk room (sleeps 4)
  • Half bath on main level
Indoor living spaces

Open-plan living flows from a double-height great room to a chef's kitchen and dining area for 12. Floor-to-ceiling glass draws the landscape inward; a floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace anchors winter evenings. A dedicated media lounge, reading nook, and mudroom with boot warmers round out the main level for gatherings that feel both spacious and intimate.

Natural timber beams, plaster walls, and locally sourced stone create a calm, tactile palette — contemporary in line, warm in material. Smart lighting and motorized shades allow you to shift the mood from bright morning coffee to candlelit dinner without leaving the sofa.

Outdoor living & wellness

Step onto a cedar deck with lounge seating, a gas fire table, and an outdoor dining table for al fresco meals. A private jacuzzi is tucked among evergreens for stargazing in every season. In summer, landscaped paths connect to a meadow overlook; in winter, the heated driveway and entry make arrivals effortless after a day on the slopes.

  • Heated private jacuzzi
  • Wraparound deck & fire table
  • Outdoor dining for 8+
  • BBQ grill & prep station
  • Heated driveway & walkway
  • Two-car heated garage
Kitchen & dining

The kitchen is fully equipped for serious cooking and effortless hosting: professional-range gas cooktop, double oven, wine refrigerator, espresso station, and a large island with bar seating. Dishwasher, microwave, and a full complement of cookware, serveware, and glassware are stocked for long stays.

A farmhouse dining table seats 12 under pendant lighting; a secondary breakfast bar seats four. We can arrange grocery delivery or private chef service on request — ask our concierge when you call to book.

Nearby experiences & seasons

Winter: World-class skiing and snowboarding within 25 minutes; snowshoeing and cross-country trails from the property boundary; après-ski dining in the village.

Summer & fall: Hiking, mountain biking, fly fishing, and scenic drives through golden aspen groves. Farmers markets, art galleries, and farm-to-table restaurants make longer stays easy to fill.

Year-round: Hot springs, wellness spas, guided wildlife tours, and stargazing from the deck — Mountain Villa is a base camp for both adventure and deep rest.
